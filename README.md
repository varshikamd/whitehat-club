# why i want to join whitehat club
I want to join the White Hats Club because I have a deep interest in ethical hacking and cybersecurity. From the moment I first learned how systems can be broken into, and more importantly how they can be protected, I’ve been fascinated by the mindset behind it. Ethical hacking isn’t just about finding vulnerabilities, it’s about thinking like an attacker so that you can build stronger defenses and keep people’s data safe. That combination of curiosity, problem-solving, and responsibility is what draws me to this field
# recent ethical hacking news
In May 2026, 19-year-old ethical hacker Nisarga Adhikary exposed serious security flaws in CBSE’s OnMark portal, which is used for on-screen marking of Class 12 answer sheets. He submitted evidence of gaining access to evaluators’ accounts and answer-sheet data through weaknesses in the system. He also found that a cloud storage bucket holding answer sheets and question papers was open without any authentication, and said he had first reported the problems in February. CBSE had earlier denied that the platform was compromised, but it later said the vulnerabilities were contained and that experts from government bodies and the IITs were securing the system
